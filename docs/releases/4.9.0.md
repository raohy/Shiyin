拾音 4.9.0：实时音频复核与提问进度
- Mac 可选择 Qwen3-ASR 实时转录，参考词汇表和前文上下文。

Shiyin 4.9.0: Live audio review and question progress
- Mac users can choose Qwen3-ASR for live transcription with glossary and preceding context.
- Local delayed audio review supplies the meeting assistant with reviewed transcripts and shows review progress; download the Qwen model and audio encoder first.
- Submitted questions appear immediately with processing stages and elapsed time, followed by complete answers and sources; failed or cancelled questions can be retried.
- Answers from another meeting no longer affect the current question list, and retries retain the original search scope.
